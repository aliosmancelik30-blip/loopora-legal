Loopora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short

  • Your subscriptions, bills, debts and income are stored only on your device. There is no account, no server of ours and no bank login.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics. We do not sell or share your data.
  • The app goes online for two things only: to fetch exchange rates, and to talk to the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore Premium.

Who we are

Loopora is made by Ali Osman Çelik, an independent developer, who is responsible for this policy. Contact: aliosmancelik30@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

What you add: names, amounts, currencies, dates and billing cycles; notes and payment-method labels; payment history; debt balances and interest rates; income amounts; and your settings. This is kept in the app's private storage and protected by your device's encryption. We never receive it.

Backups you control

  • iCloud (iPhone): when iCloud backup is on in Loopora's settings, a copy of your Loopora data is saved in your own iCloud storage so it comes back if you reinstall the app. Apple keeps it under your Apple Account; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in the app.
  • Android backup: depending on your device settings, Android can include Loopora's data in your Google account backup. Google keeps it; we cannot access it.
  • Backup file: "Save a backup file" creates a file on your device. Where you keep or send it is up to you.

Bank statement import

If you import a CSV statement, the file is read only on your device and the file itself is not stored. Only the subscriptions you confirm, and the matching statement lines (date, description, amount) as proof, are kept on your device.

Services the app connects to

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
frankfurter.dev (open-source exchange rates)When you pick a main currency, and at most twice a dayOnly a currency code such as "USD". As with any web request, the service can see your IP address.
Apple App Store / Google PlayWhen you buy, restore or check PremiumHandled by the store. We never receive your payment details; the purchase is verified on your device.

Your list of subscriptions, bills, debts and notes is never sent to any of these services. Service logos are built into the app, so no logo service learns which services you use.

Reminders and app lock

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; their content never passes through a server. If you lock the app with Face ID or fingerprint, your device checks it; we never receive biometric data.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export as CSV, or Settings → Backup → Save a backup file.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data on the device. Turn off iCloud backup in the app first if you also want the iCloud copy removed.
  • Purchases are kept by Apple or Google. Use your store account to cancel or ask for a refund.

Children

Loopora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
