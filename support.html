Contact

Premium

Manage or cancel Premium in your store account: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. On a new phone, open Loopora → Settings → Restore purchases.

Moving to a new phone

  • iPhone: with iCloud backup on, your data comes back when you install Loopora with the same Apple Account.
  • Android: your data is restored from your Google account backup when you reinstall.
  • Any phone: Settings → Backup → Save a backup file on the old phone, then "Restore from a backup file" on the new one (also offered on the welcome screen).

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Loopora in your phone's settings, and that Settings → Notifications is on in the app.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data.
